Lean Swiss Burger with Sweet Potato Wedges and Strawberry SaladLunch, Monday

about 42 min

Imported from Lean_Swiss_Burger_Night.pdf

INGREDIENTS

STEPS

FREEZING

- Form 4 patties and dent the centers. Freeze flat with parchment between them. - Freeze on a parchment-lined tray until firm, then move to a bag so they don't stick together. Cook or reheat straight from frozen. - Store the Swiss separately rather than freezing it on the patties. - Cooked meat and fish keep best for 2-3 months. Reheat until steaming hot all the way through. - Label with the name and date. Best within 2-3 months. - Thaw overnight in the fridge, or reheat from frozen. Doesn't freeze well: - tomato: fresh tomato goes soft; fine cooked into a sauce, otherwise add fresh - lettuce leaves: doesn't freeze (goes limp and watery); add it fresh when serving
